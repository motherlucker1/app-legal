FAQ

I got a new phone. Can I get my purchases back?
Open the shop in the game and tap "Restore purchases" while signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account. Subscriptions are restored this way. Progress itself is stored on the device.

How do I cancel the Vigil Pass?
iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Android: Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends to avoid the next charge.

Where is my data stored?
Only on your device. We do not run servers or collect personal data. Uninstalling the game deletes your progress.
