Tinystep Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Tinystep ("the app") takes your privacy seriously. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

1. We don't collect personal information

The app requires no sign-up and collects no personal information such as your name, phone number, email, location or contacts. Your habits and check-ins are stored only on your device and are never uploaded to any server.

2. Purchases

On iOS and Android, purchases are handled by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We never receive your payment information.

3. Notifications

If you turn on reminders, the app schedules local notifications on your device. They never pass through any server.

4. Backup and deletion

You can export a backup or erase all data anytime in Settings. Uninstalling the app also deletes all local data.

5. Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13.

6. Contact

Questions? Email xuxing@xingzhun.org.

小步打卡 隐私政策

更新日期：2026 年 11 月 23 日

「小步打卡」（以下简称“本应用”）非常重视你的隐私。本政策说明本应用如何处理你的信息。

1. 我们不收集个人信息

本应用不要求注册登录，不收集姓名、手机号、邮箱、位置、通讯录等任何个人信息。你创建的习惯和打卡记录仅保存在你的设备本地，不会上传到任何服务器。

2. 内购与支付

在 iOS / Android 版本中，购买由 Apple App Store 或 Google Play 处理，我们不会获取你的支付信息。

3. 通知

如果你开启提醒，本应用会在你的设备上设置本地通知，不经过任何服务器。

4. 数据备份与删除

你可以随时在「设置」中导出备份文件，或「清空所有数据」。卸载应用也会删除全部本地数据。

5. 儿童隐私

本应用不面向 13 岁以下儿童收集任何信息。

6. 联系我们

如有疑问，请联系：xuxing@xingzhun.org
