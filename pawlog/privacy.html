Pawlog Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

1. We don't collect your data

Everything you enter in Pawlog (pet profiles, photos, health entries, reminders, expenses) is stored only on your own device. It is never uploaded to our servers, and we cannot see it.

2. Photos

Photos you choose for your pets are compressed and stored on your device, used only to display them in the app and to create pet ID cards.

3. Notifications

Reminders use your device's local notifications and never pass through any server. You can turn them off anytime in your device settings.

4. Purchases

In-app purchases are handled by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We never receive your payment information.

5. Deleting data

Go to Me → Erase all data to delete everything. Uninstalling the app also deletes all local data.

6. Children

This app does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13.

7. Contact

Questions? Email xuxing@xingzhun.org.

毛孩日记 隐私政策

更新日期：2026 年 11 月 23 日

1. 我们不收集你的数据

你在毛孩日记中录入的所有信息（宠物资料、照片、健康记录、提醒、花费）都只保存在你自己的设备上，不会上传到我们的服务器，我们也无法看到。

2. 照片

你为宠物选择的照片会被压缩后保存在本机，仅用于在 App 内显示和生成宠物名片。

3. 通知

提醒功能使用设备的本地通知，不经过任何服务器。你可以随时在系统设置中关闭。

4. 购买

应用内购买由 Apple App Store 或 Google Play 处理，我们不会获得你的支付信息。

5. 数据删除

在「我的 → 清空所有数据」可以删除全部数据；卸载 App 也会删除所有本地数据。

6. 儿童

本 App 不面向 13 岁以下儿童收集任何信息。

7. 联系我们

如有问题请发邮件至 xuxing@xingzhun.org。
