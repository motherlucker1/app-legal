FAQ

How many free uses do I get?
The free plan includes a few runs every day. Pro removes the limit.

I got a new phone. Can I get Pro back?
Open Settings and tap "Restore purchases" while signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account.

How do I cancel?
iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Android: Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends to avoid the next charge.
