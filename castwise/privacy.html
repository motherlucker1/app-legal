Privacy Policy · Castwise

Last updated: 2026-11-24

Castwise ("the app") does not require you to sign up and does not ask for your name, email address or phone number. To count free uses and link your subscription, the app creates a random anonymous ID on your device (shown in Settings). It is not linked to your identity.

Content you submit

When you tap Generate, the transcript you entered and your chosen options are sent over an encrypted connection to our server, which passes them to our AI provider (Anthropic) to write the result. We do not keep your content after the result is returned, do not sell it, and do not use it to train AI models. Your history is saved only on your device, and you can delete the app to remove it.

Subscriptions

Subscriptions are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We never see your payment details. To unlock Pro features, the app sends the store receipt to our server, which checks it with Apple or Google. You can manage or cancel a subscription in your App Store or Google Play account settings.

Advertising and tracking

The app shows no ads and does not track you across other apps or websites.

Feedback

If you choose to send feedback from the Settings screen, the message you write, the email address you optionally enter, and basic technical details (app version, platform and language) are sent through Web3Forms (web3forms.com) to our support inbox. Nothing is sent unless you press Send. We use it only to reply and improve the app, and you can ask us to delete it by email.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

隐私政策 · Castwise

《Castwise》不需要注册，不收集姓名、邮箱、手机号。为了统计免费次数和关联订阅，App 会在设备上生成一个随机匿名 ID（设置里可见）。

你点“生成”时，输入的内容经加密连接发到我们的服务器，再交给 AI 服务商（Anthropic）撰写结果；生成后不保留、不出售、不用于训练模型。历史记录只保存在你的设备上。

订阅由 App Store / Google Play 处理，我们只把购买凭证发给苹果或谷歌校验，看不到支付信息。App 不含广告，也不做跨应用跟踪。
