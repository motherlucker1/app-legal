Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Glimmer Slide does not require an account and does not collect your name, phone number or email address. Your game progress is stored only on your device.

Ads

The game shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your device's advertising identifier (IDFA / GAID), approximate location and device information to show and measure ads. On iOS the game asks for your permission to track first, and you can decline. Users in the EU and UK see a consent screen. See Google's advertising policy.

In-app purchases

Purchases are handled by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We never receive your payment information.

Children

The game does not target personalized ads at children under 13.

Feedback

If you send us a message through the in-app feedback form, the text you write, your email address (only if you choose to enter it), and basic app details (app name, version, platform, language) are sent to us by email through Web3Forms, a form delivery service. We use it only to read and answer your feedback. Nothing is sent unless you tap Send.

Contact

Questions? Email xuxing@xingzhun.org.

隐私政策

更新日期：2026-11-23

《萤石归位 Glimmer Slide》（以下简称“本游戏”）不要求注册，不收集姓名、手机号、邮箱等个人信息。游戏进度只保存在你的设备本地。

广告

本游戏使用 Google AdMob 展示广告。AdMob 可能会收集设备广告标识符（IDFA / GAID）、大致位置和设备信息，用于展示和衡量广告。iOS 上会先征求你的跟踪授权，你可以拒绝；欧盟/英国用户会看到同意设置。详见 Google 广告政策。

应用内购买

购买由 Apple App Store 或 Google Play 处理，我们不会接触你的支付信息。

儿童

本游戏不面向 13 岁以下儿童定向投放个性化广告。

意见反馈

如果你通过 App 内的“意见反馈”给我们留言，你写的内容、你选择填写的邮箱，以及基本的 App 信息（名称、版本、平台、语言）会通过表单投递服务 Web3Forms 以邮件形式发给我们，仅用于阅读和回复你的反馈。你不点“发送”，就不会发出任何内容。
