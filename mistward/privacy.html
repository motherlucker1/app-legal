Privacy Policy · Mistward

Last updated: 2026-11-24

Mistward ("the game") does not require an account and does not collect your name, email address, phone number or other personal information, except what you choose to include when you send feedback. Your progress is stored only on your device.

Advertising

The game can show optional rewarded ads and occasional ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your device's advertising identifier (IDFA / GAID), approximate location and device information to show and measure ads. On iOS you are asked for tracking permission before the first ad and you can decline; users in the EEA, UK and Switzerland see a consent form. Subscribers do not see ads. See how Google uses data in ads.

In-app purchases and subscriptions

Purchases, including the optional VIP Pass subscription, are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We never see your payment details. You can manage or cancel a subscription in your App Store or Google Play account settings.

Feedback

If you choose to send feedback from the Settings screen, the message you write, the email address you optionally enter, and basic technical details (app version, platform and language) are sent through Web3Forms (web3forms.com) to our support inbox. Nothing is sent unless you press Send. We use it only to reply and improve the app, and you can ask us to delete it by email.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
