Privacy Policy · Tetherwing

Last updated: 2026-11-23

Tetherwing ("the game") does not require an account and does not collect your name, email address, phone number or other personal information. Your progress is stored only on your device.

Advertising

The game shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your device's advertising identifier (IDFA / GAID), approximate location and device information to show and measure ads. On iOS you are asked for tracking permission first and you can decline; users in the EEA, UK and Switzerland see a consent form. See how Google uses data in ads.

In-app purchases and subscriptions

Purchases, including the optional Tether Pass subscription, are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We never see your payment details. You can manage or cancel a subscription in your App Store or Google Play account settings.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

隐私政策 · 光索战机

《光索战机 Tetherwing》不需要注册，不收集姓名、邮箱、手机号等个人信息，游戏进度只保存在你的设备上。

广告由 Google AdMob 提供，AdMob 可能收集设备广告标识符（IDFA / GAID）、大致位置和设备信息用于展示和衡量广告；iOS 会先征求跟踪授权，你可以拒绝。

购买（包括可选的「光索通行证」订阅）由 Apple App Store 或 Google Play 处理，我们接触不到你的支付信息；订阅可随时在账户设置中管理或取消。
